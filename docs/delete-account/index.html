Delete your FCN Fans account

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This page applies to FCN Fans.

App and developer identification

App: FCN Fans
Developer support: fcnfans.support@gmail.com

Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your account and request deletion of the associated personal data directly in FCN Fans:

  1. Open FCN Fans and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon labelled Profil in the header.
  3. On Min Profil, scroll to the bottom and tap Slet konto.
  4. Confirm the deletion by tapping Slet konto again.

When deletion is complete, the app signs you out. This action cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app

Email fcnfans.support@gmail.com and state that you want your FCN Fans account and associated personal data deleted. Please identify the account concerned so support can help with your request.

What data is deleted

Data directly associated with the account is normally deleted, including:

  • the sign-in account and profile information;
  • posts, comments, likes and poll votes;
  • match check-ins, RSVPs and fan-activity registrations;
  • community memberships and account-linked fan-faction requests;
  • notification settings, push-token records and notification records;
  • files and uploads owned by the account; and
  • messaging data directly linked to the account.

What data may be retained

Some shared or administrative records may remain without being linked to your deleted account. For example, a group conversation may remain available to its other members. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

For more information, read the FCN Fans privacy policy.
